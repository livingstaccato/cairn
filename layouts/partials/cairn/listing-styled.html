A row of filenames under the listing read as more files in the directory, which is the opposite of what they were. Everything here is present before any script runs. The filter ships hidden and cairn.js reveals it, so a visitor without JavaScript sees a complete listing and no dead controls. */ -}} {{- $c := .Params.cairn -}} {{- /* Entries come from the index.json resource in this page's bundle, not from frontmatter. Nothing is generated twice, which is where the earlier drift between the two came from. */ -}} {{- $r := .Resources.GetMatch "index.json" -}} {{- $entries := slice -}} {{- with $r }}{{ $entries = (. | transform.Unmarshal).entries }}{{ end -}} {{- /* The page renders at most max_rendered rows.
